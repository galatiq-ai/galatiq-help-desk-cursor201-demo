# Brazos Ridge team wiki (fictional)

Brazos Ridge Electronics is a fictional distributor. These seven pages stand in for the team wiki a real service desk would keep in Confluence, Notion or similar. In this workshop they are plain markdown files, so **you attach them with `@`** (for example `@wiki/routing-and-triage-standards.md`) and Cursor reads them like any other file. Nothing here is real company policy.

- [Brazos Ridge Cloud Platform: Storage and Retention Standard](storage-and-retention-standard.md)
- [Brazos Ridge IT: AI Agent Usage Policy](ai-agent-usage-policy.md)
- [Brazos Ridge Security Operations: Incident Intake](security-incident-intake.md)
- [Brazos Ridge Service Desk: Key Accounts and Trading Partners](key-accounts-and-trading-partners.md)
- [Brazos Ridge Service Desk: Priority, SLA and Escalation Matrix](priority-sla-and-escalation-matrix.md)
- [Brazos Ridge Service Desk: Release Log and Known Issues](release-log-and-known-issues.md)
- [Brazos Ridge Service Desk: Routing and Triage Standards](routing-and-triage-standards.md)

The runbooks (what to do about a specific failure) are in `runbooks/`. The wiki says how the team works; a runbook says how to fix one thing.
